Chapter I.

Behind the Code

With over eight years in my craft, I bridge the user's actions to the infrastructure that answers them. My work consists of refactoring old code, generating CMS ecosystems, or producing greenfield SaaS solutions.

As a solutions architect, I transcribe complex business requirements into lucid, technical frameworks that put user flow in the forefront. My specialization is adapting systems to growth and establishing a clear product to teams who inherit them.

I conceptualize architectural answers through various mediums. These engagements range from solo architecture consulting to leading cross-functional teams through multi-phase delivery. With the rapid evolution of the digital landscape, I carefully consider how each piece integrates with one another, encapsulating usability with a level of flexibility only honed through high pressure environments.
